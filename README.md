Thaumic Tinkerer: Reborn is a complete rewrite of Thaumic Tinkerer for Thaumcraft 6. The mod introduces a lot of misc content that don't really belong to a specific theme, making it perfect if you just want extra Thaumcraft 6 content. New content was also added, some of it originating from older versions of Thaumic Tinkerer.

Unlike the original, KAMI's content has been split off into its own addon, Thaumic Tinkerer is no longer required to access any of it.

---

## Devices
The Transvector Interface is completely optional and can be turned off in the config if it's too overpowered.

- **Attractors (Anima/Corporeal/Kinetic):** Pulls items/entities/players towards it (depending on the attractor type). Sneaking and right-clicking the attractor changes the polarity.
- **Dissimulation Block:** A powerful block that allows the player to change it to any other block's appearance.
- **Ethereal Platform:** Passable from the side, but not from the top. Sneak and right-click to completely go through it. Perfect for elevators and the Arcane Levitator.
- **Paving Stone of Permeability:** Right-clicking it with a gauntlet will temporary make it act like a liquid. When multiple blocks are together, they will all act in sync when activated.
- **Transvector Dislocator:** Teleports blocks and entities on top of it within a 3x3x3 area to another linked dislocator when activated with redstone. Linking two dislocators requires a Transvector Binder.
- **Transvector Interface:** Multiple faces on this block can be connected to faces of other blocks using a Transvector Binder, but only within a limited range. It is able to accept items, fluids, or redstone power.

## Equipment

- **Feline Charm:** Any player wearing this will make creepers afraid to be near them.
- **Infusion Enchantment: Educational:** Each level of this infusion will further boost the amount of experience received from slaying creatures or breaking blocks.
- **Infusion Enchantment: Projecting:** The infusion will add an additional +0.5 Reach Distance to the tool per level.
- **Infusion Enchantment: Wrath:** The infusion will first apply a base damage of +0.5 to the tool, and then add an additional +0.5 damage per level. It is also compatible with all normal enchantments.
- **Ignium Striker:** A Flint and Steel upgrade that never breaks and uses Vis.
- **Ring of Goliath's Might:** Reduces total damage dealt to the player by 1. It will break and save the player from death if they are dealt a killing blow (does not protect when dying to fall damage).
- **Sigil of Consuming:** When worn and activated, it will instantly destroy any material picked up. Sneaking and right-clicking will activate or deactivate.
- **Sword of the Condor:** An upgrade to the Sword of the Zephyr, right-clicking will make the player charge towards a direction (sneaking and right-clicking will reverse the direction). Using it several times will allow the player to fly around.
- **Talisman of Experience:** When worn and activated, it will store experience within itself, right-clicking will extract the stored experience. Sneaking and right-clicking will activate or deactivate.

## Misc

- **Aquatic Alchemy:** Extra alchemy recipes to create more Prismarine Shards, Sponges, and Sea Lanterns.
- **Arcane Quartz:** A new darker quartz variety used for decorative blocks and several recipes throughout the mod.
- **Ender Mirror:** Any player can access their Ender Chest inventory by right-clicking it!
- **Energetic Nitor:** Has to ability to place temporary nitor to light up the area. When activated, it also places them automatically in dark areas. Sneaking and right-clicking will activate or deactivate.
- **Escape Sigil:** Teleports the player vertically to the first block exposed to the sky when right-clicked. Gets consumed in the process.
- **Essentia Funnel:** Allows much more quick transporting between essentia jars. Just place the funnel directly on top of a hopper connected to another essentia jar.
- **Everfull Bucket:** This bucket will never run out of water! At the cost of Vis.
- **Obsidia Terra:** A much tougher obsidian that is 2.5 times more resistant, but also allows easier harvesting. Great for building fortified bases!
- **Thaumic Cake:** Simply eat a slice of this cake and it will regenerate itself!
- **Thaumic Restorer:** When a damaged tool is placed on it (by right-clicking with it on the device), it will start restoring it at the cost of essentia. Tools will cost Instrumentum, weapons will cost Aversio, and armor will cost Praemunio.
- **Thaumium Plated Cobblestone:** A cheap but more resistant building block.
- **Timetwister Stopwatch:** When right-clicked on a location, it will start counting down from 60 seconds. When the timer is up, it will instantly teleport you back to that location, and restore previous hunger and health values from that state.
- **Umbral Roses:** You can plant a rose bush in order for it to grow, then use shears to collect individual roses.

## Necromancy
All of Necromancy's content are completely optional and can all be turned off in the config if they're too overpowered.

- **Necromancy Tablet:** The tablet can be constructed into a multiblock that lets you summon various mobs using a catalyst, ingredients, and essentia. Some recipes will not consume ingredients and can be repeated for automating mob farms.
- **Souls:** There are seven different soul types in total (Peaceful, Hostile, Arcane, Demonic, Alien, Tainted, and Eldritch). Souls are used as the main catalyst for summoning mobs, and have a durability for each time they get used.
