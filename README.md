Thaumic Tinkerer: Reborn is a complete rewrite of Thaumic Tinkerer for Thaumcraft 6. The mod introduces miscellaneous content that does not belong to a specific theme, making it perfect if you just want extra Thaumcraft content.

Unlike the original, KAMI's content has been split off into its own addon separate from Thaumic Tinkerer. You can find this new mod here: [KAMI: Reborn](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/kami-reborn).

---

## Devices
- **Attractors (Anima/Corporeal/Kinetic):** Pulls items/entities/players towards it (depending on the attractor type). Sneaking and right-clicking the attractor changes the polarity.
- **Dissimulation Block:** A block that can mimic the appearance and behavior of other blocks.
- **Ethereal Platform:** Passable from the underneath or from the side, but not from the top. Sneaking allows players to sink through the top of the block. Perfect for elevators and the Arcane Levitator.
- **Paving Stone of Permeability:** Right-clicking with a gauntlet will temporary make the block act like a liquid. When multiple blocks are together, they will all activate in sync.
- **Transvector Dislocator:** Teleports blocks and entities on top of above the block within a 3x3x3 area to a linked dislocator.
- **Transvector Interface:** Link the each face of the interface to any other block. Linked faces will mimic tile entity capability, allowing insertion or removal of fluids, items or redstone flux from a distance.

## Equipment

- **Feline Charm:** When equipped, causes nearby creepers to flee in terror.
- **Ignium Striker:** Vis powered Flint and Steel.
- **Ring of Goliath's Might:** Reduces damage taken by 1 and protects the user from death and shatters if the user has more than 6 health.
- **Sigil of Consuming:** When activated, instantly destroys any trash material harvested, such as cobblestone or netherrack.
- **Sword of the Condor:** An upgraded Sword of the Zephyr, launching the player in the direction they are facing when right-clicked (sneaking will reverse the direction).
- **Talisman of Experience:** When equipped, absorbs any experience the player picks up and stores it within the amulet. When activated, drains experience from the player and stores it within the amulet. Stored experience can be retrieved by right-clicking while holding the amulet.

## Enchantments
- **Infusion Enchantment: Educational:** Increases experience dropped when slaying enemies or breaking blocks.
- **Infusion Enchantment: Projecting:** Increases reach distance by +0.5 per level.
- **Infusion Enchantment: Wrath:** Increases the base damage of a weapon by +0.5 plus an additional +0.5 damage per level. This damage stacks with the Sharpness enchant.

## Misc

- **Aquatic Alchemy:** Extra alchemy recipes to create Prismarine Shards, Sponges, and Sea Lanterns.
- **Arcane Quartz:** A new darker quartz variety used for decorative blocks and recipes throughout the mod.
- **Ender Mirror:** Allows players to access their Ender Chest inventory.
- **Energetic Nitor:** Has to ability to place temporary light sources. When activated, it automatically places light sources in dark areas.
- **Escape Sigil:** Teleports the player vertically to the first block exposed to the sky when right-clicked. Item is consumed in the process.
- **Essentia Funnel:** A hopper addon that allows rapid transfer of essentia out of jars.
- **Everfull Bucket:** Uses vis to generate water source blocks.
- **Obsidia Terra:** Reinforced obsidian with 2.5 times the blast resistance of obsidian.
- **Thaumic Cake:** Regenerating cake that grants Regeneration I when consumed.
- **Thaumic Restorer:** Repairs damaged items using essentia. Tools are repaired with Instrumentum, weapons with Aversio, and armor with Praemunio.
- **Thaumium Plated Cobblestone:** A cheap but more resistant building block.
- **Timetwister Stopwatch:** When used, stores the player location and stats. After 60 seconds, the player will teleport back to that location restoring their hunger and health to previous values.
- **Umbral Rose Bush:** A plant that grows and can be sheared for Umbral Roses, which can be crushed into black dye.

## Necromancy
All Necromancy content is optional and can be disabled using the configuration.

- **Necromancy Altar:** An altar used to summon entities using essentia, reagents and an entity soul. Includes CraftTweaker, GroovyScript and JEI support.
- **Entity Souls:** There are seven different soul types in Thaumic Tinkerer; Peaceful, Hostile, Arcane, Demonic, Alien, Tainted, and Eldritch. Souls are the main consumable reagent used to summon entities.
